### LeetCode 835 — Algorithm Steps

1. `img1` aur `img2` ke **saare `1` coordinates** identify karo.
2. `img1` ke har `1` ko `img2` ke har `1` ke saath pair karo.
3. Har pair ke liye shift calculate karo:

   * `rowShift = row2 - row1`
   * `colShift = col2 - col1`
4. `(rowShift, colShift)` ko **ek single shift** samjho.
5. Map mein us shift ki frequency increment karo.
6. Har increment ke baad maximum frequency maintain karo.
7. **Maximum frequency = maximum overlap.**

**Complexity:** `O(n⁴)` time, `O(n²)` space.
